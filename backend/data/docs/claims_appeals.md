# North Star Health Plan — Claims and Appeals

This is a fictional policy used for demonstration only.

## Filing a claim
In-network providers file claims on the member's behalf.
For out-of-network services, the member must file a claim within 180 days of the date of service.

## Explanation of benefits
After a claim is processed, the member receives an Explanation of Benefits showing the billed amount, the allowed amount, the plan payment, and the member's responsibility.

## Appeals
A member may file an internal appeal within 180 days of receiving a claim denial.
The plan decides standard internal appeals within 30 days for services not yet received and within 60 days for services already received.
Urgent appeals are decided within 72 hours.
If the internal appeal is denied, the member may request an external review by an independent review organization within 4 months.
