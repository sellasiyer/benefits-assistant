# North Star Health Plan — Pharmacy Benefit and Formulary

This is a fictional formulary used for demonstration only.

## Drug tiers
Tier 1 covers preferred generic drugs with a $10 copay for a 30-day supply.
Tier 2 covers preferred brand-name drugs with a $40 copay for a 30-day supply.
Tier 3 covers non-preferred brand-name drugs with a $80 copay for a 30-day supply.
Tier 4 covers specialty drugs with 30% coinsurance up to a maximum of $250 per 30-day supply.

## Mail order
A 90-day supply through mail order costs two times the 30-day retail copay for Tier 1 through Tier 3 drugs.

## Step therapy
Some Tier 3 drugs require step therapy, meaning the member must first try a lower-tier alternative.
A provider may request a step therapy exception if the alternative is not medically appropriate.

## Formulary exceptions
Members may request coverage of a drug that is not on the formulary through a formulary exception request.
Formulary exception requests are decided within 72 hours, or within 24 hours when expedited.
