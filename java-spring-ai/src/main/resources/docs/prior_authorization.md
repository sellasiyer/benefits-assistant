# North Star Health Plan — Prior Authorization Policy

This is a fictional policy used for demonstration only.

## What requires prior authorization
Prior authorization is required for MRI, CT, and PET scans performed outside an emergency.
Prior authorization is required for inpatient hospital admissions that are planned in advance.
Prior authorization is required for specialty drugs on Tier 4 of the formulary.
Prior authorization is required for durable medical equipment costing more than $500.

## How to request prior authorization
The treating provider submits the request through the provider portal or by fax.
Standard requests are decided within 15 calendar days.
Expedited requests, used when waiting could seriously harm the member's health, are decided within 72 hours.

## If prior authorization is not obtained
If a required prior authorization is not obtained, the claim may be denied.
The member may appeal the denial following the claims appeal process.
