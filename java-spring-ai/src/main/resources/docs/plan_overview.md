# North Star Health Plan — Gold PPO 2026 Summary of Benefits

This is a fictional plan used for demonstration only.

## Deductible
The in-network deductible is $1,500 per individual and $3,000 per family per plan year.
The out-of-network deductible is $3,000 per individual and $6,000 per family.
Preventive care is covered before the deductible is met.

## Out-of-pocket maximum
The in-network out-of-pocket maximum is $5,000 per individual and $10,000 per family.
Once the out-of-pocket maximum is reached, the plan pays 100% of covered in-network services for the rest of the plan year.
Premiums and non-covered services do not count toward the out-of-pocket maximum.

## Copays and coinsurance
A primary care office visit has a $25 copay.
A specialist office visit has a $50 copay.
An urgent care visit has a $75 copay.
An emergency room visit has a $350 copay, which is waived if the member is admitted to the hospital.
After the deductible, in-network coinsurance is 20% and out-of-network coinsurance is 40%.

## Preventive care
Annual physicals, routine immunizations, and recommended cancer screenings are covered at 100% in network with no copay.
